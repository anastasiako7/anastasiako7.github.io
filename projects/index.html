Projects

  • Ariel Space Mission:
    • Co-Coordinator of Ephemerides working group on Ariel and of the ExoClock project, involving over 2000 professional and amateur astronomers from 65 countries around the world, with the aim of monitoring transiting exoplanets.
    • Coordinator of the ArielEDU program aiming to promote exoplanet activities and the Ariel mission in schools.
  • Open Science and Citizen Science:
    • Creator of ExoWorlds Spies, an open platform aiming to provide educational material and exoplanet research to amateur astronomers, schools and the general public.
    • Founder of ExoClock Unlocked, a project aiming to provide opportunities and training to members that don’t have access to observing facilities.
  • Pulsar Non-Profit Civil Company:
    • Co-Founder of Pulsar, a non-profit civil company based in Greece aiming to open science to diverse communities.
    • Organizer of outreach events, educational seminars, and science activities designed for schools and the general public.
