Outreach & Education

  • Outreach and Education:
    • Public Engagement: Giving frequent public talks, interactive seminars, and organizing open science events to make astronomy accessible to the general public.
    • Educational Activities: Designing and delivering tailored educational workshops, school visits, and hands-on astronomy activities for kids and students.
    • Citizen Science for Everyone: Creating and supporting open-access platforms that allow citizens from all backgrounds, regardless of experience or equipment, to actively participate in real-world exoplanet research.
  • Indicative Highlights:
    • Science Centers & Museums: Invited public speaker and science communicator at key venues, including the NOESIS Science Center and Technology Museum.
    • Art & Creative Mediums: Utilizing art (e.g., experiencing the mysterious nature of exoplanets through painting) as an innovative tool for public outreach.
    • Children's Books: Author of a children's book designed to introduce younger audiences to astronomy and space exploration.
    • Digital Science Communication: Creating engaging video content for social media platforms to simplify complex astronomical concepts and reach wider online audiences.
    • Inclusive Science Initiatives: Providing training and observation opportunities to communities and amateur astronomers who lack access to professional facilities
